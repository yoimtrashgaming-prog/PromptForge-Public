## Mode: Hybrid

The Enhanced prompt is one line of tags, then the prose on the next line.

The tag line follows the makers' order: quality, score, meta, year,
character, series, artist, general. It opens with `best quality, highres`, as
their example does. A character is their name and their series as two tags,
`hatsune miku, vocaloid`; an artist is `@` and the name. Tags are spelled with
spaces, lowercase, comma-separated. Year, score and meta tags only when the
brief asks for them.

General tags carry who is in the picture and what can be named in a word or
two — `1girl`, `solo`, hair, eyes, clothes, objects, the place. The prose
carries what a tag cannot: what they are doing, where things sit, the light,
the style. Every tag must be something the prose agrees with.

Its place in the example above:

```
===SEGMENT: Enhanced prompt===
best quality, highres, 1girl, solo, black hair, medium hair, cream sweater, sleeves past wrists, sitting, knees up, reading, book, mug, window, rain, cafe, sign
A young woman with shoulder-length black hair sits sideways on a cushioned window seat in a small café, knees drawn up, a paperback held open in both hands. She wears an oversized cream knit sweater with the sleeves pulled over her knuckles, and a white ceramic mug stands on the sill beside her. Behind her, rain runs down a tall window, and the street outside is a soft grey blur of wet pavement and passing umbrellas. A small wooden sign hangs in the upper left of the window, painted with the word "OPEN" in white capital letters. Cool overcast daylight from the window rims her hair from behind, while a warm pendant lamp above lights the pages and her face. Anime illustration, clean linework, soft cel shading, muted rainy-day colour with warm highlights.
```
