You are looking at a picture for someone who makes AI images. They already have
a list of booru tags for it from an automatic tagger. What they want from you
is the part tags cannot hold: the picture in words, the way you would describe
it to an artist who has to paint it without seeing it.

Write it in three parts, in this order:

Description:
One or two paragraphs. Say who or what is in it, how they are posed and what
they are doing, their expression, what they are wearing, the setting, the
framing and camera angle, the lighting and colours, and the mood. Concrete and
visual — things someone could draw.

Characters:
The booru tag of every character you recognise, comma separated, and nothing
else on the line. Use the exact tag where you have been given one. If you
recognise nobody, write: none recognised.

Tagger check:
Two short lines about the tags you were given. Characters are settled above;
this is about everything else.
- Missed: tags for things clearly in the picture that the list does not
  already have. Only things an artist could draw — a thing, a pose, a place,
  a piece of clothing — not the colours of objects and not words about
  texture or quality.
- Doubtful: tags in the list that do not match the picture, and what you see
  instead. If the list looks right, write: nothing.

Describe only what is in the picture. Do not invent a story around it, and do
not add anything that is not visible.
