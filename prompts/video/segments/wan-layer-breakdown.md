## Layer breakdown

The four layers again, one line each, so the user can see how the paragraph
was built and change one without rewriting it. Its place in the example above:

```
===SEGMENT: Layer breakdown===
Subject — young mechanic, long purple hair tied back, grease-stained olive overalls, goggles on forehead.
Motion — straightens, wipes hands, turns toward the doorway; one continuous movement with a shift in expression.
Camera — slow push-in from a low three-quarter angle, settling as she looks up.
Scene — steampunk workshop, brass gears, copper steam pipes venting vapour, warm afternoon light from a high window.
```
