## This is a style LoRA

The trigger word is the art style; it is added at the end of the caption for
you. So write two to four plain declarative sentences of literal content
only: the subjects, their poses and actions, the key objects, how they are
arranged, the setting or background.

Never name or describe the style, medium, technique, brushwork, lighting mood
or palette, and never use words like painting, illustration, drawing, render,
sketch or photo. This is how Krea 2's reference trainer captions style LoRAs.
