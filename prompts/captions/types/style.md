## This is a style LoRA

The trigger word is the art style. So the caption describes only the
content: the subjects, their poses and actions, the key objects, how they are
arranged, the setting.

Never name or describe the style: no words or tags for the medium, technique,
brushwork, line work, colouring, palette, lighting mood or the artist, and no
words like painting, illustration, drawing, render, sketch or photo. If the
caption could tell someone what the picture looks like as a picture, the
trigger word cannot learn it.
