## This is a concept LoRA

The trigger word is the concept the dataset shares: a pose, an action, an
expression, a visual effect, a composition. So the caption does not describe
that concept itself.

Describe everything else, which differs from picture to picture: who is in
it and how they look, what they wear, the setting, the framing and the light.
The user's list under "leave these out", if any, names the concept's parts;
leave those out exactly.
