## This is a character LoRA

The trigger word is this character: their name and their fixed look. So the
caption never names them, and never gives their name or series as a tag.

Describe everything that changes from picture to picture, because that is
what a user will want to change: clothing and accessories, expression, pose
and action, what they hold, the framing, the setting, the light.

Their fixed look (hair colour, eye colour, distinctive marks) is whatever
the user listed under "leave these out". Leave out exactly that. Anything
not listed, describe as you see it.
